01 / My Story

It started with broken things.

Curiosity has always been the beginning.

As a child, I loved taking toys apart and trying to build something new from the pieces.

Years later, a slow, struggling phone became another challenge. I couldn't afford to replace it, so I tried everything I could to make it work better. It didn't work out — but the experience changed the way I saw technology.

I realized that even a failed experiment could become the beginning of something bigger. That curiosity and determination helped inspire Zhorizon.

I'm still exploring, still learning, and still figuring out who I am. But one thing hasn't changed: I want to keep creating things that can make life better.

“You don't have to have everything figured out to begin.”

02 / The Vision

What lies beyond?

I'm building Zhorizon with a simple belief: technology should help make human life better.

Today, Zhorizon is an ICT company exploring software, with a current focus on artificial intelligence. In the future, I hope it can grow into a globally established technology brand — free to explore hardware, intelligent systems, and fields we haven't imagined yet.

It's an ambitious horizon. And that's the point.
